Featured GenAI Cross-Platform Offline AI

D&D Scene Generator

Flutter · whisper.cpp · llama.cpp · stable-diffusion.cpp · Dart FFI · C/C++

A cross-platform app that listens to spoken tabletop RPG narration and generates images of the scene in real-time — 100% offline, on-device. Speech is transcribed with whisper.cpp; a local LLM (llama.cpp) detects new locations from the narrative; stable-diffusion.cpp generates an image from a prompt built from the scene description, your chosen art style, and character likenesses.

  • Three separate on-device AI models coordinated in a real-time pipeline
  • Runs on Windows, Linux, Android, and iOS — same codebase
  • Model selector: choose speed vs quality trade-off per device
  • PhotoMaker integration for consistent character likenesses across scenes
  • Custom Dart FFI layer binding to stable-diffusion.cpp across all platforms
